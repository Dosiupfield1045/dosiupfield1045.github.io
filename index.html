🎮 What Is This Tool?

NBA 2K27 MyCAREER Badge Planner is a lightweight, concept-stage Windows application created for players who want to approach their MyCAREER journey smartly. It helps you organize your badge progression, compare attribute distributions across different player builds, and keep records of your gaming sessions – all in one clean interface. Please note: this tool is an independent fan-made concept and does not modify game files or make any claims about badge unlock mechanics. It's purely a planning and organizational companion.

Whether you're trying to figure out how many points to allocate toward Playmaking or you want to look back at your progression over the last month, this app gives you the clarity you need. Stop juggling spreadsheets and sticky notes – let the planner do the heavy lifting.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app official? Does it unlock badges for me?

No. This is an independent planning tool created by a fan (Dosiupfield1045). It does not modify your game, connect to NBA 2K27 servers, or guarantee any badge unlocks. It is purely for tracking and comparison. The game itself still requires you to earn badges through gameplay.

Will my data be sent anywhere?

Absolutely not. All data stays on your local machine. There are no analytics, no telemetry, and no internet connection required. Your privacy is fully protected.

Is the app free?

Yes, it's completely free to use. This is a passion project. You can donate to the developer if you wish, but it's never required.

Does the app work on Mac or Linux?

Currently, only Windows is supported. Mac and Linux users would need to run it through a virtual machine or Wine, but it's not officially tested for those environments.

I found a bug. What do I do?

Please report bugs on the GitHub repository's Issues page. Include a screenshot and a brief description of what happened. The developer typically responds within a few days.
